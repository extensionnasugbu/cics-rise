Needs Assessment and Baseline Data

Survey of 367 respondents, mostly 4Ps members: low income, little online work experience, and beginner-level digital skills in every project area.

Community context (PSA and DSWD records): 1,256 females in Nasugbu remain unemployed, and 1,522 4Ps beneficiaries are identified, 89.49% of them female.
